# Verbos irregulares

> **Legenda de cores:** <span style="color:#3b82f6">azul = sujeito</span> · <span style="color:#22c55e">verde = verbo/ação</span> · <span style="color:#eab308">amarelo = complemento/detalhe</span>

Verbos que **não** ganham `-ed` no passado. Não há regra — é decoreba. A boa notícia: cerca de 50 cobrem quase tudo que você fala no dia a dia.

Colunas: **base** (infinitivo) → **past** (Simple Past, ver [[simple-past]]) → **participle** (present perfect e voz passiva).

## Grupo 1 — as três formas iguais (as mais fáceis)

| Base | Pron. | Past | Pron. | Participle | PT |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| cut | *cât* | cut | *cât* | cut | cortar |
| put | *put* | put | *put* | put | colocar |
| set | *sét* | set | *sét* | set | definir/configurar |
| let | *lét* | let | *lét* | let | deixar/permitir |
| hit | *rit* | hit | *rit* | hit | bater/atingir |
| cost | *cóst* | cost | *cóst* | cost | custar |
| read | *ríd* | read | ***réd*** | read (*réd*) | ler |
| shut | *chât* | shut | *chât* | shut | fechar |

> ⚠️ **Erro comum:** `read` se escreve igual nas três formas mas o passado se pronuncia ***réd***, não *ríd*.
> `I read every day` (*ái **ríd** évri dêi*) / `I read it yesterday` (*ái **réd** it iésterdei*).

| go | *gôu* | went | *uênt* | gone | *gón* | ir |
| do | *du* | did | *did* | done | *dân* | fazer |
| see | *sí* | saw | *só* | seen | *sín* | ver |

| give | *guív* | gave | *guêiv* | given | *<span style="color:#ef4444">guí</span>vân* | dar |
| come | *câm* | came | *kêim* | come | *câm* | vir |
| know | *nôu* | knew | *niú* | known | *nôun* | saber/conhecer |
| write | *ráit* | wrote | *rôut* | written | *<span style="color:#ef4444">rí</span>tân* | escrever |
| speak | *spík* | spoke | *spôuk* | spoken | *<span style="color:#ef4444">spôu</span>kân* | falar |
| break | *brêik* | broke | *brôuk* | broken | *<span style="color:#ef4444">brôu</span>kân* | quebrar |

| begin | *bi<span style="color:#ef4444">guín</span>* | began | *bi<span style="color:#ef4444">guén</span>* | begun | *bi<span style="color:#ef4444">gân</span>* | começar |
| show | *chôu* | showed | *chôud* | shown | *chôun* | mostrar |

> ⚠️ **Erro comum:** o `k` de `know` e o `w` de `write` são **mudos**. `know` = *nôu*, `wrote` = *rôut*.

## Os que mais aparecem no seu dia (dev)

| Frase (cores) | Pronúncia | Português |
|---|---|---|
| <span style="color:#3b82f6">I</span> <span style="color:#22c55e">ran</span> <span style="color:#eab308">the tests</span>. | *ái rén dâ tésts* | Eu rodei os testes. |
| <span style="color:#3b82f6">We</span> <span style="color:#22c55e">built</span> <span style="color:#eab308">the image</span>. | *uí bílt di <span style="color:#ef4444">í</span>midj* | Nós buildamos a imagem. |
| <span style="color:#3b82f6">The deploy</span> <span style="color:#22c55e">broke</span> <span style="color:#eab308">prod</span>. | *dâ di<span style="color:#ef4444">plói</span> brôuk pród* | O deploy quebrou a prod. |
| <span style="color:#3b82f6">I</span> <span style="color:#22c55e">wrote</span> <span style="color:#eab308">the migration</span>. | *ái rôut dâ mai<span style="color:#ef4444">grêi</span>chân* | Eu escrevi a migration. |
| <span style="color:#3b82f6">I</span> <span style="color:#22c55e">found</span> <span style="color:#eab308">the bug</span>. | *ái fáund dâ bâg* | Eu encontrei o bug. |
| <span style="color:#3b82f6">She</span> <span style="color:#22c55e">sent</span> <span style="color:#eab308">the PR</span>. | *chí sént dâ pi-<span style="color:#ef4444">ár</span>* | Ela mandou o PR. |
| <span style="color:#3b82f6">We</span> <span style="color:#22c55e">got</span> <span style="color:#eab308">a 500 error</span>. | *uí gót â fáiv-<span style="color:#ef4444">râd</span>red <span style="color:#ef4444">é</span>rôr* | Nós recebemos um erro 500. |
| <span style="color:#3b82f6">He</span> <span style="color:#22c55e">made</span> <span style="color:#eab308">a mistake</span>. | *rí mêid â mis<span style="color:#ef4444">têik</span>* | Ele cometeu um erro. |

> ⚠️ **Erro comum:** aplicar `-ed` em irregular. ~~*goed, maked, buyed, breaked, writed*~~ → **went, made, bought, broke, wrote**.

> ⚠️ **Erro comum:** usar o **participle** no lugar do **past**. ~~*I have went*~~ → *I have gone*. ~~*I seen it*~~ → **I saw it** (*ái só it*).
